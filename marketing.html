Alpha Chi Omega, Columbia University

As VP of PR & Marketing I own our chapter's public presence: the content calendar, the visual identity, and the voice behind every post. That means planning the shoots, taking the photos, editing the graphics, writing the captions, and making sure recruitment, philanthropy, and day-to-day chapter life all look like they belong to the same brand.

The goal is a feed that feels like the chapter actually feels — warm, a little playful, and consistent enough that you recognize it before you read the handle.
