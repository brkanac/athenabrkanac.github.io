Video Work

Nostalgia · a video essay.

Nostalgia will be the death of me.

Nostalgia will be the death of me. I have often thought this in my head while scrolling back through the thousands of photos in my camera roll. With this year being my 20th birthday, I have been feeling even more sentimental towards my teenage years.

I created this piece as an exploration of these feelings. I wanted to encompass the feelings of looking at the past but also bring attention to the problems of dwelling. I was mourning something that I didn't seem to appreciate in the moment. But I also wanted to emphasize that I am not alone. I wanted to open up the narrative to include how my feelings of nostalgia are not unique. We are all longing for the past.

To critique these feelings, I wanted to focus on the negatives as well. When looking back at the past, we only seem to remember the things that brought us joy. We forget the pain, the sadness, and the hard times we went through.

So many terrible things happened in 2016, and we should keep that in mind when we talk about how “awesome” it was back then.

Looking back can feel comforting, but it can also distort what was really there. Through this piece, I wanted to capture both the beauty and the danger of romanticizing the past.

Made for Lab in the Video Essay with Professor Behrang Garakani, a course exploring the power of visual essays.
