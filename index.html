03 — ABOUT

A little
about me. ☆

Hi! I'm Athena. I am a student currently based in New York City.

Being from Seattle, Washington, I have a great love for the outdoors. In my free time, I enjoy embarking on sunrise hikes, doing various arts and crafts, and collecting a wide array of Hello Kitty memorabilia.

Whether I am behind the camera or developing a creative strategy, I want my work to feel thoughtful, expressive, and memorable. I am inspired by visionaries Sofia Coppola, Wong Kar-Wai, Hirokazu Kore-eda, and Kai Fusayoshi
